## Domain evidence and differences from rain shells

Research `.local/product-width/research/softshell_jackets.md` compares
[Rab Borealis](https://rab.equipment/uk/mens-borealis-hooded-jacket) and
[Mammut UltimateVIII SO](https://www.mammut.com/uk/en/products/1011-02670-0001/ultimate-viii-so-hooded-jacket-men).
Single-weave stretch nylon differs from a membrane laminate with separate stretch
panels/inserts. A softshell is not necessarily membrane-free, fleece-backed or
fully seam-taped. Rab273g sizeM and Mammut559g without a stated size are not
interchangeable measurement bases. No customer outcomes or sampling probabilities
are copied from these examples.

## Authored dependency recipe

The neutral apparel base supplies positioning/execution, nominal adult alpha
labels, colours and sparse canonical colour/size-label manufacture. Joint body
construction: stretch woven nylon without a membrane or separate lining (weight4,
250g centre); or polyester face/polyethylene membrane/open3D polyester backing with
separate stretch panels (weight3,480g). These generic assemblies are authored
bounded proposals, not exact complete source compositions; stretch fibre percentage
and panel geometry are unspecified. No dedicated insulating fill is declared.
Fleece-backed softshells remain deferred rather than silently invented.

Wind declaration is a soft conditional draw: woven wind-resistant:windproof6:1;
membrane1:6. A membrane is therefore not a perfect label shortcut. Neither label
is an air-permeability result. DWR present:absent4:1, independent of wind declaration;
seam treatment and numeric waterproof result remain unspecified. Do not inherit
the rain-shell taped-seam or hydrostatic-head/MVTR menus.

Hood absent/fixed/adjustable weights1:2:4; attached whenever present, no detachability,
stowing or helmet-fit inference. Underarm zip vents yes:no1:4 for woven,3:1 for
membrane. Cut slim/regular/relaxed2:5:1, without asserting anatomical fit.
One synthetic sizeM garment mass = construction centre+20g if hood exists+15g if
vents exist+uniform integer noise±20g. Price bands SEK500–1100,1000–2200,1900–3800,
round10. Masses/prices/weights are authored, not source calibration; no SKU-specific
mass or price effect. Retail listing/stock remain downstream.

Optional RET declaration present:absent1:2, independent of body construction. A
published claim is explicitly **RET <20 or <30m²·Pa/W**, weights1:1, not an exact
20/30 result or an MVTR value. Values are authored declarations; test method and
conditioning remain unknown. An unpublished claim has an inactive numeric envelope;
physical breathability remains unknown, not zero or not applicable to the garment.

## Public facts and customer evidence

Describe body components/lining, membrane presence, separate stretch panels,
unquantified stretch, cut/front zip, hood/adjustability, vents/DWR, wind declaration,
unknown seam/waterproof/test details, optional bounded RET claim and reference mass.
A fixed hood must not be called adjustable. Unknown seam details do not mean no
sealing; no membrane in the authored woven assembly is a fact, not an inference
from a source page omitting the field.

Shared concentration1.5 stars,75% silent ratings, exact tested canonical SKU,
variable topics/order and price-dependent value policy. Three generic non-value
meanings remain relevant even without hood/vents: perceived wind comfort during
a short dry breezy walk with front closed; arm-movement comfort over a light layer
in the tested size; front-entry-zip operation. Hood-adjustment ease requires an
actual adjustable hood; underarm-vent operation requires vents. Wind comfort is
not certified windproofness or temperature safety. Movement comfort is not a
measured stretch percentage. Hardware difficulty does not imply failure, and easy
vent operation is not an RET/MVTR result. No rain-dryness meaning is inherited.

## Acceptance and explicit deferrals

Retain seeds0–19. Check both body assemblies, all hood/vent/publication branches,
soft wind-label support, RET inequality/units and missing claims, mass arithmetic,
sparse alphabetic SKU identity/grouping and conditional wording rejection.
Predicates for membrane, adjustable hood and declared wind category are proposals
only, rejecting missing requests. RET threshold consumers remain deferred until
they explicitly handle upper bounds and unknown method/conditioning.

Other deferrals: measured body/gender/cut conversion, stretch/air-permeability tests,
rain exposure, care/wear effects, fleece-backed assembly, storm flaps, pocket/thumb
loop details, numeric thermal outcomes and physical construction-dependent reviews.
No connected-world, task/grader, deployed page or laboratory-equivalence claim.
