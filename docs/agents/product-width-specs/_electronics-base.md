# Electronics colour manufacture module

Status: prose draft before DSL, 2026-10-01. Supporting authoring base, not a concept.
Shared only by current additive electronics packages. No existing pilot or compiler
change; physical/electronic capability facts and reviews belong to each concept.

Canonical model identity, independent positioning/execution, authored colour subset
and one manufactured SKU per chosen colour. Colour selector or separate-page
presentation are retailer grouping policies; no manufacture/listing/stock mixing.
No inherited clothing sizes, shoe widths, mass basis, connection, battery, charging,
noise control or universal physical attributes. A headphone is not automatically
wireless, rechargeable or ANC-equipped because of its family.

Colour subset black/white/silver/blue weights5:3:2:2, counts1/2/3/4 weights35:40:20:5.
All selected colours produce canonical product_id-colour identity; unselected
colours are not manufactured. One product may have only one colour. Retailer can
carry any sparse subset or none and can retain sold-out carried colours. No assumed
cross-model/common manufacturer palette, firmware/configuration equivalence,
regional stock or source-exhaustive variant enumeration. Recipe palettes/weights are
tunable proposals rather than empirical market frequencies.

Positioning uses supplied brand weights; execution independent uniform.05–.95.
Reference SEK price supplied by concept-specific bands, with no retailer pricing
or colour surcharge. Current brand fixtures are explicit authoring inputs; family
registry and generic preview do not establish production compatibility or brand
scope adoption. The public projection exposes canonical facts; private profile and
draw probabilities remain outside it. No field called Wh is populated from hours.
