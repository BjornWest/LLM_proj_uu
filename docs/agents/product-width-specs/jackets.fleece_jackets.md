## Authored recipe and dependencies

Neutral apparel positioning/execution, nominal alpha sizes, colours and sparse
canonical colour/size manufacture. Both bounded body assemblies are polyester
knitted fleece, without a membrane, separate body lining or dedicated fill. Joint
construction: open-grid fleece backer (weight3; areal-mass choices170/180/190g/m²;
whole-garment centre300g) or sweater-knit face with fleece back (weight2;
320/340/360g/m²; centre600g). Choices within either fabric menu are equally weighted.
This soft cross-construction mass association is a recipe; fabric weight does not
compute jacket mass from an invented area or establish thermal resistance.

Cut slim/regular/relaxed2:5:2. Full front zip and stand collar fixed; no hood in this
first recipe. Hand-pocket construction none/open/zipped weights1:2:5 jointly sets
0/2 pockets and no/open/zip closure. Whole-garment reference mass is one nominal
sizeM jacket, construction centre+15g when hand pockets exist+uniform integer
noise±25g. These mass/feature effects are authored, not measured or source-normalized.
Price bands SEK300–800,700–1400,1200–2400, round10. SKU-specific mass/price deferred.
No retailer stock/offer/destination result enters manufacture.

Anti-pilling treatment, numeric pilling grade, thermal rating and quantitative
waterproof rating are explicitly unspecified. Unknown treatment is not untreated;
no membrane is a fact of this authored assembly, not an inference from an omitted
source field. No inherited rain-shell taped seams, softshell wind labels or RET.

## Public content and customer meanings

Describe fibre, knit structure, absent separate body lining/membrane/fill, areal
and whole-garment mass with different units/bases, cut, collar, entry and actual
pocket construction. A0-pocket product must not describe an existing pocket's
closure. Nominal size labels do not imply measured body/gender/cut conversion.

Shared concentration1.5 stars,75% silent ratings, exact tested canonical SKU,
variable topics/order and price-dependent value policy. Original meanings:
perceived sufficient warmth during a short cool dry walk over a light layer with
front closed; arm-movement comfort in the tested size over a light layer; front
entry-zip operation; noticeable body-fabric surface pilling after10 washes; value.
Warmth is not a safe-temperature rating. Movement comfort is not stretch percentage.
Zip difficulty is not failure. Pilling is not shedding, fading, holes or seam damage,
and does not infer washing error or fibre cause. The10-wash context is an authored
experience, not source testimony or a standardized abrasion/pilling protocol.

## Acceptance and deferrals

Retain seeds0–19; inspect both construction domains, all pocket assemblies, mass
arithmetic, areal/garment-unit separation, unknown treatment/ratings, absence facts,
sparse canonical grouping and faithful review paraphrases. Numeric maximum
declared fabric mass, structure and hand-pocket predicates are proposals, rejecting
missing requests; no connected renderer/filter/task/grader adoption.

Deferred: fibre blends/recycled fractions, anti-pill options, standardized grades,
thermal physics, hooded/half-zip forms, pocket/trim materials, garment measurements,
gender/cut charts, care history and SKU-dependent mass. Opinions are synthetic,
not guaranteed physical construction-dependent outcomes or harvested reviews.
