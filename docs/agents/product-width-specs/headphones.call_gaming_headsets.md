# Call/gaming headsets — bounded executable authoring recipe

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Concept
`headphones.call_gaming_headsets`; family `headphones`. Boom-microphone headsets
for compatible-computer audio. A microphone is distinct from wearer ANC, and the
headset's supply differs from a host-powered wireless receiver.

## Assembly and capability dependencies

Four joint templates: analogue stereo over-ear2; USB-audio mono on-ear2;
USB-audio stereo on-ear2; wireless-receiver stereo over-ear3. All closed dynamic
headset constructions with a supplied adjustable boom microphone.40mm drivers in
the on-ear USB templates,50mm in the over-ear templates. Driver diameter does not
establish incoming or microphone quality, sound localization or competitive gain.
Analogue source plug is explicitly stereo/headset4-pole3.5mm CTIA. USB audio uses
a USB-A/USB-C host plug equally; wireless has a matching USB-A/USB-C receiver
equally. Connector choices are model configurations, with no complete regional or
UC/MS/application-certification matrix inferred.

Analogue headset is passive with no storage battery; the boom requires a compatible
host microphone input but is not a rechargeable headset. USB-audio headset is
host-powered, with no storage battery; USB-C here carries audio. Wireless headset
has rechargeable storage, matching-receiver audio and optional Bluetooth2:1, with
Bluetooth5.2/5.3 when present. Its headset USB-C connector charges only; no USB audio
or analogue playback in that template. No simultaneous mixing is supported by this
recipe. Included microphone operation is declared only on the primary audio route;
Bluetooth microphone routing is unspecified, not silently supported or absent.
Receiver power from the host is separate from how the headset itself is supplied.
Wearer ANC is absent in this first recipe; measured isolation/speech processing,
latency/IP protection and firmware/host integration remain unspecified.

Boom attachment non-removable/removable: analogue1:2, USB2:1, wireless1:1. Both
are adjustable; non-removable does not mean rigid. Mute is a button by default,
inline on analogue and on the ear cup on USB/wireless. Non-removable USB booms may
instead raise-to-mute/lower-to-unmute, equally with button mode. This is a declared
default method, not a universal feature or proof of application-wide mute. Removable
booms do not inherit flip-mute. All represented volume controls are buttons at the
same inline/earcup location, distinct from a flip-mute boom's mute action.

Wireless music maximum over matching receiver15/30/50h equally, declared empty-charge
time2/3h via headset USB-C. Nominal/usable cell energy and battery protocol unknown;
no hours or capacity without voltage are called Wh. Battery/charging/Bluetooth
quantities are not applicable where their capability is absent. No calls maximum,
Bluetooth runtime, quick charging or receiver mass is inferred.

Headset reference mass excluding cables, receiver, charger and removable boom but
including a non-removable boom: analogue300g, USB mono110g, USB stereo190g,
wireless330g, plus8g when the boom is non-removable, integer noise±10g. These are
authored reference assemblies, not harmonized source measurements. One headset
mass is not per ear cup or an accessory bundle. Reference SEK bands250–900,
800–2000,1800–4500, round10; colour-only manufacture has no seller stock/quotes.

## Original customer experiences

Shared concentration1.5,75% silent ratings and modest cheap-price value boost.
Generic non-value meanings: wearing comfort30min seated at a desk; perceived clarity
of familiar spoken audio in a quiet room at usual comfortable level; preference for
one's own voice after recording the same short script through the supplied boom
on the represented primary route to a compatible computer and replaying it.
Incoming speech and outgoing recorded-voice preference remain distinct. The latter
is a direct recording experience, not a claim that another caller heard clearly.
No stereo localization is assigned to mono, and no comment guarantees low latency,
gaming wins, full silence, hearing protection or private calls.

Actual host plug/receiver connection handling, volume buttons and the represented
mute operation have context-bound ease/difficulty meanings. Microphone attachment
handling requires a removable boom. Bluetooth pairing requires represented Bluetooth
and a compatible device; it does not claim Bluetooth mic routing. Charging-connector
handling applies only to rechargeable wireless headsets and is not proof of charge
or USB playback. Every statement binds the exact canonical colour headset SKU and
retains polarity; accessories and left/right cups are not extra manufactured SKUs.
