# Hiking boots — domain specification

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Registry concept
`footwear.hiking_boots`. Adult enclosed hiking footwear with a mid or high collar;
height describes construction, not injury protection or universal ankle support.

Primary examples: [Merrell Moab 3 Mid Waterproof](https://www.merrell.com/US/en/moab-3-mid-waterproof/52470M.html?dwvar_52470M_color=J038195)
declares suede/mesh and an arch shank;
[Salomon Quest 4 GORE-TEX](https://www.salomon.com/en-us/product/quest-4-gore-tex-lg4311)
has regular laces and a support chassis;
[LOWA Renegade Evo GTX Mid Wide](https://www.lowaboots.com.au/products/renegade-gtx%C2%AE-mid-wide-copy)
uses a PU midsole and states pair mass. Research is retained in
`.local/product-width/research/hiking_boots.md`. None supplies our synthetic EU40
mass calibration. Loaded-hike marketing is not tested customer performance.

## Distinct construction

Reuse independent positioning/execution and soft cushioning/lightness/support
emphasis, but no running-session profile. Named bundles jointly select upper,
midsole material, sole structure, cushioning and reference mass: suede/textile
with EVA/moderate/500 g; leather/textile with EVA/substantial/620 g; nubuck with
PU/moderate/690 g. Each has a rubber hiking outsole. Positive emphasis weights
retain every bundle. PU versus EVA does not prove stiffness or durability.

Collar height (mid/high) is sampled separately; high adds an authored 50 g. Noise
is uniform ±25 g; membrane presence adds 20 g. These are tunable synthetic
one-boot EU40 reference masses, not conversions of unspecified manufacturer
measurements. Use regular adjustable laces in this initial recipe; alternative
closures are deferred, not impossible. Quick lacing is not inherited from the
low hiking shoe. Nominal lugs are 4 or 5 mm. Reference price bands overlap:
SEK 700–1500, 1400–2500, 2200–4000 across budget/mainstream/premium.

Arch shank, toe reinforcement, gusseted tongue and waterproof membrane have
separate booleans. Toe protection is not an underfoot rock plate; an arch shank
is not a named support chassis. A gusset may help limit debris but does not
certify water exclusion. Keep waterproof test rating unknown. Detailed chassis,
rock plate, stiffness, fractional drop, pack-load capability and crampon
compatibility remain deferred. No seller claim becomes an injury/safety fact.

## Manufacture, descriptions and evidence

Nominal adult size/colour/width manufacture and exact SKU attribution reuse the
neutral module. A change to collar/construction/membrane is another model here.
Retailer carried subsets, stock and selector/page choices remain later decisions.
Half sizes, extra-wide widths, conversion charts and size-dependent mass are
deferred. Descriptions disclose collar, intended hiking use, upper, midsole,
outsole/sole structure, cushioning, regular lacing, lugs, protection components
and reference mass. Proposed predicates request collar height or membrane;
neither is automatically registered with tasks or grading.

Review meanings cover day-hike comfort, forefoot fit, uncomfortable collar
rubbing during day hikes, uncomfortable lace pressure while walking uphill
and downhill on hikes, and purchase value. Both alternatives of a wording pool
preserve the same context and outcome: rubbing never invents blisters, lace
pressure never invents a broken closure, day-hike comfort never certifies loaded
multi-day trips. SKU identity is exact but individual foot shape/pack load remain
outside this bounded opinion recipe. Preserve 75% rating-only default,
concentration1.5 and the accepted modest fixed-satisfaction price/value effect.

## Acceptance

Keep all fixed seeds 0–19. Test collar/mass and bundle coupling, independent
component compatibility, nonzero emphasis weights, canonical sparse manufacture,
fact-bound wording and review scope. Do not search for seeds or convert empty
