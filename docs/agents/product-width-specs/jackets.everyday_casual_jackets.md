## Authored recipe and dependencies

Nominal alpha sizes/colours and sparse canonical manufacture come from the apparel
base. Joint assemblies: denim trucker (weight3;100%cotton denim, unlined body,
point collar, front buttons, open hand pockets, garment centre750g); Harrington
(weight2;58%polyester/42%cotton woven shell, polyester body lining, dog-ear collar,
zipped front, buttoned hand pockets, centre500g); canvas chore (weight3;100%cotton
canvas, unlined body, corduroy point collar, front buttons, open hand pockets,
centre850g). Complete shell fibre percentages sum100; named collar material is a
local component, not the complete shell. A corduroy structure does not specify
fibre composition, which remains unknown. Assembly labels/closures/lining are
bounded authored combinations, not universal style definitions. Denim's unlined
body and all numeric garment centres are author choices, not source inference.

Two hand pockets fixed; no total-pocket count or complete pocket layout claim.
Dedicated fill, membrane and hood absent in this scope. Surface finish, numeric
fabric areal mass, stretch percentage, waterproof/thermal/abrasion ratings remain
unknown. Cotton content does not imply zero mechanical stretch. Cut slim/regular/
relaxed2:4:3. One-way/two-way zip1:3 when zipped entry; no two-way function for
front buttons. Cuff adjustment yes/no1:1, without asserted adjustment geometry.
Two-button collar fastening yes/no3:1 only for the dog-ear collar; other collars
have no represented collar fastening. These tunable choices do not copy every
control from the original source models.

One nominalM reference jacket mass=assembly centre+integer noise±50g. No areal
fabric calculation, source-normalized mass or size/cut conversion. Price bands
SEK600–1500,1300–2600,2400–4800, round10. Manufacture remains separate from retail
stock/pricing/shipping, and no mixed-climate performance is inferred from material.

## Public facts and exact-SKU experience

Describe construction, body-fibre composition, actual lining/collar/local material,
entry/hand-pocket closure, available collar/cuff/two-way controls, explicit absence
facts and unknown measured results. Front buttons and collar buttons are separate.
Shared concentration1.5,75% silent ratings and modest price-dependent value policy.
Generic authored first-hand topics: arm/shoulder movement comfort in tested size
over a light shirt; sufficient warmth on a short mild dry walk over a light shirt
with front closed; operating the actual front fastening; value. Front fastening
paraphrases bind to buttons or zip, not a nonexistent control, and difficulty does
not imply breakage. Conditional topics: cuff adjustment when present; operating
the two collar buttons when that fastening exists; lower zip slider opening the
front from below only for a two-way zip. No inherited fleece pilling, fill power,
insulation metrics, rainy-use protection or quantified temperature guarantees.

## Acceptance and limits

Retain seeds0–19; check all three assemblies, both zipped functions and conditional
cuff/collar features. Verify shell totals, lining versus unspecified properties,
nominalM mass basis, faithful operation wording and exact canonical tested SKU.
Construction, lining and maximum reference garment mass predicates are unregistered
proposals; missing requests reject. No production task/filter/renderer/grader
adoption is claimed.

Deferred: leather/suede, padded/sherpa/hooded/stretch-blend variants, exact fit/gender/
body charts, measured fabric area/mass, secondary-shell placement, laundering,
colour retention, care/break-in physics, shape geometry, SKU-dependent mass/prices
and physical construction-dependent reviews. Source review aggregation, sweepstakes
or tall-size testimony does not expand manufactured variants or calibrate outcomes.
