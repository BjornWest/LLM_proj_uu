# Low hiking shoes — domain specification

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Registry concept
`footwear.low_hiking_shoes`. Adult enclosed, low-collar footwear intended for day
hikes on ordinary trails. These are not running shoes or climbing footwear, and a
low collar neither promises ankle support nor implies that hiking is unsafe.

Primary examples: [Merrell Moab 3](https://www.merrell.com/US/en/moab-3/52481M.html?icid=search_suggested_products)
declares suede/mesh, a nylon arch shank and 5 mm lugs;
[Salomon X Ultra 5 GORE-TEX](https://www.salomon.com/en-us/product/x-ultra-5-gore-tex-li6285/L47725500)
declares low height and quick lacing;
[KEEN Targhee IV](https://www.keenfootwear.co.uk/products/mens-targhee-iv-waterproof-java-bison)
declares nubuck, a membrane, toe bumper and stability shank. The latter page's
mixed shoe/boot reviews cannot identify exact low-shoe SKUs. Detailed observations
are in `.local/product-width/research/low_hiking_shoes.md`.

## Construction and shopper facts

Reuse nominal adult sizing and sparse colour/width manufacture, independent
positioning/execution and the existing soft cushioning/lightness/support emphasis.
Select a coherent construction bundle: light textile, mixed suede/textile or a
sturdier leather upper, with a foam midsole and lugged rubber outsole. The bundle
sets upper, nominal cushioning and reference mass together. Emphasis changes
weights without prohibiting any bundle. These are plausible authored recipes,
not market frequencies or tests of support/performance.

The light, mixed and sturdy recipes have authored reference masses 360/440/500 g,
with uniform ±25 g noise. Their cushioning is moderate/substantial/modest. Sample
membrane presence independently and add 20 g when present. Reference price ranges
are SEK 450–1100 (budget), 1000–1900 (mainstream), 1700–3200 (premium), with
overlap and no retailer-service interpretation.

Sample laces or quick-lace cord, toe reinforcement and a declared waterproof
membrane separately. A membrane adds a small authored reference-mass increment.
Do not equate leather with waterproofing or toe reinforcement with an underfoot
rock plate. Waterproof test rating remains explicitly unknown even with a
membrane. A whole-shoe mass uses the synthetic one-shoe EU40 basis; weights from
real examples with another/unstated basis are not relabelled.

Nominal lug depth is a separate model fact. Declared traction construction cannot
prove wet-rock grip. Keep detailed stiffness grades, rock-plate construction,
certified waterproof tests, multi-day heavy-load suitability and crampon
compatibility deferred rather than inventing guarantees.
An arch shank is a separate declared component, independent of the toe bumper;
neither is an underfoot rock plate. Heel-to-toe drop is deferred, so the running
package's integer grid does not silently exclude the inspected 11.5 mm example.

## Manufacture and evidence

Size/colour/width identify exact manufactured SKUs. Retailer selectors versus
separate pages, subset listing and stock remain later decisions. Extra-wide and
half sizes, size-conversion charts and size-dependent mass remain shared deferrals.
Model construction, membrane and closure changes produce another model here.

Descriptions disclose intended use, low collar, upper/sole/cushioning, fastening,
lug depth, toe reinforcement, arch-shank presence, membrane presence and reference mass. Proposed
predicates can request a membrane or a maximum reference mass; they do not
register tasks or filters in a live world.

First-hand review meanings cover comfort during day hikes, forefoot fit, heel
rubbing during day hikes, experienced traction on dry loose paths, and purchase
value. Preserve context and polarity across formulations. Comfort on these hikes
does not establish suitability for backpacking, and a heel-rubbing complaint must
not turn into an invented blister/injury. All reviews bind one canonical tested
SKU. Star concentration1.5, 75% rating-only default, protected value frequency and
the accepted modest price/value adjustment remain shared policy.
