## Public facts and exact-SKU experience

Show actual wearing geometry, closed back/dynamic driver/diameter, each connection
capability, actual control/microphone/ANC facts, applicable battery quantities with
units/contexts and explicit unknowns. Never advertise digital USB audio from USB-C
charging, nor calls through passive cable from Bluetooth mic. No acoustic fidelity,
waterproofness, universal host compatibility or occupational safety guarantee.
Concentration1.5,75% silent ratings and shared price/value policy remain unchanged.

Generic authored first-hand meanings: wearing comfort after60min seated indoors;
perceived vocal clarity on a familiar playlist at usual comfortable listening level;
preference for its perceived bass balance on that playlist/level; value. These are
subjective distinct meanings, not objective accuracy or safe-exposure measurements.
Conditional topics: Bluetooth pairing to a compatible phone; operating the actual
ear-cup volume buttons/touch controls; connecting/disconnecting detachable analogue
cable; perceived background-noise reduction seated on a train with ANC enabled.
Each requires its represented capability, exact tested colour SKU and faithful
polarity/context. A microphone or battery does not automatically receive call,
charging, runtime or ANC testimony. No inferred driver failure from disliked bass.

controls/ANC/mic and applicable battery declarations. Verify cellV/mAh/Wh arithmetic,

omitting manufacturer page. Nominal energy is not usable energy or battery lifetime.
