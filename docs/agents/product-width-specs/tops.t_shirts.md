## One-shirt owner histories and natural language

An owner selects one canonical SKU uniformly and tests only its first included
shirt. The actual `review.subject` is the whole selected purchase and
`review.tested_shirt_id` binds that same included shirt. The private complete history
records an adult wearing it during the first afternoon at home, unlayered against
skin, then washing that same shirt gently at30°C and line drying fully. The shirt
is laid flat to observe its neckline trim. Labels/parts are unaltered. A count2
purchase leaves one shirt unworn and unwashed; never extend that observation to
both, another size/colour, repeated care, exercise, sleep or somebody else's body.
No measurements, drying-time trial, adverse-reaction diagnosis or causal explanation
are invented. Compatible history is complete before eligibility/rating/topics.

Four eligible aspects: whole-pack value, fabric feel during first wear, neck feel
during first wear, and neckline appearance after first care. Both polarities are
possible for either recipe under the same compatible history. The neckline report
is an attributed visual opinion, not measured shrinkage, elasticity or lifetime.
Ratings use shared concentration1.5. Topic-count weights0:24/1:4/2:3/3:1 give75%
silent owners, without seed filtering; all subsets up to three topics are legal.
Value retains the protected shared0.30 mass (execution modulation at budget/
premium), with equal remaining nonvalue mass. Favourability uses
`normal((stars−1)/4−0.2*(relative_price−0.5),sd0.22)`, clipped0..1 and quantized
at0.000001; positive iff at least0.5. Other topic-positive probability is
`0.08+0.84*(stars−1)/4`. Weights/spread are authored policy, not source evidence.
