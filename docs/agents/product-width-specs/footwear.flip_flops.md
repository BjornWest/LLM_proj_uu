# Flip-flops — domain specification

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Registry concept `footwear.flip_flops`.
Initial scope is adult thong footwear with a toe post, open toe/heel and no rear
or ankle strap. A molded plantar heel area is not an enclosed upper heel. Slides
and back-strapped sandals are different constructions, deferred here.

[Havaianas Top Max](https://www.havaianas-store.com/eur/en/havaianas-top-max/4140449.html)
declares PVC straps and a rubber sole; the inspected photograph shows fixed
Y-shaped thong contact. Its paired labels are Brazilian, not two EU SKUs.
[BIRKENSTOCK Gizeh Essentials EVA](https://www.birkenstock.com/us/gizeh-eva/gizeh-eva-eva-0-eva-u_3716.html)
declares EVA components, contoured footbed and adjustable instep buckle. Its
“Regular/Wide” label does not prove two widths. Observations and retained contact
photographs are in `.local/product-width/research/flip_flops.md`; no individual
review outcomes or measured masses were established.

## Construction and manufacture

Jointly choose PVC strap/rubber footbed/outsole (reference160g) or EVA strap/
footbed/outsole (reference110g). Fixed thong versus adjustable instep buckle and
flat/textured versus contoured footbed are separate conditional draws: both are
possible in either material bundle, with soft preferences. Do not make the material
an infallible shortcut for adjustability or contour. These unobserved combinations
are explicit authored proposals; no monolithic one-piece construction is asserted.

Mass basis is one synthetic EU40 item, with uniform ±10g and15g buckle increment.
No source mass or physical material effect calibrates these values. Positioning
and execution stay independent; reference-price bands overlap at SEK100–350,
250–650 and500–1300. Nominal adult EU size/colour/width grids are authored
manufacture, not translations of the Brazilian pairs or a brand width label.
Half/paired sizes, conversions and measured widths remain deferred.

Catalog facts state toe-post contact, exposed toe/heel, no rear/ankle strap,
component materials, closure, footbed shape and reference mass. Shape does not
promise arch support or gait correction. Waterproof test rating stays unknown;
open/water-friendly footwear does not keep the foot dry or certify wet-tile grip.
Height/drop/platform, adjustment range and quantitative traction are deferred.
Retailer grouping, listing subsets, prices and stock remain later decisions.

## Customer evidence and task proposals

Applicable authored experiences cover comfort during short casual wear, toe-post
pressure during short casual wear, uncomfortable strap rubbing at the top of the
instep during short casual wear, and purchase value. No enclosed-heel/collar rubbing
is inherited. Plantar heel contact exists but has no review pool in this recipe.
Pressure is not rubbing, rubbing is not blisters, and short wear does not certify
long-distance, shower/beach or wet-floor use.

Buckle adjustment is a separate opinion eligible only for an actual adjustable
instep buckle. It concerns ease of that adjustment, not foot fit or buckle
reliability. Select applicability before topics, retain exact tested SKU, and reject
wrong-mechanism wording. Shared75% silent default, concentration1.5 and modest
fixed-satisfaction price/value effect remain unchanged.

Proposed predicates request closure or footbed shape; no production filter/task
registration follows. Validate fixed seeds0–19, all material/closure compatibility
branches, contact/exposure facts, sparse canonical manufacture, and scoped wording.
Do not infer market frequencies from two examples or discard sparse/no-match draws.
Adoption remains isolated authoring preview, with old hosts and pilots untouched.
