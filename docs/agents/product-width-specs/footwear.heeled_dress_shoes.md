# Heeled dress shoes — domain specification

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Registry concept
`footwear.heeled_dress_shoes`. The initial recipe covers court shoes, slingbacks
and ankle-strap dress sandals. Nominal heel heights start above the preceding
recipe's provisional 30 mm boundary; this is an authoring partition, not universal
fashion terminology. Platforms, wedges, mules and other styles remain deferred.

Examples: [Clarks Kataleyna Gem](https://www.clarks.com/en-nl/kataleyna-gem/26171219-p)
declares a slip-on court shoe and 70 mm slim heel (cached extraction);
[Clarks Kataleyna Step](https://www.clarks.com/fr-fr/kataleyna-step/26187612-p)
declares a 70 mm slingback with adjustable rear buckle;
[Naturalizer Vera](https://www.naturalizer.com.au/products/vera-dress-sandal-turtledove-snake)
declares an open toe, ankle buckle and 63.5 mm block heel, but not outsole material.
Research limits are in `.local/product-width/research/heeled_dress_shoes.md`.
Do not turn that example's EVA insole into an EVA outsole, or a footbed thickness
into heel height/drop/platform height. No inspected mass calibrates this recipe.

## Construction and manufacture

Joint style bundles determine toe shape, toe/heel exposure, strap position, closure
and authored reference mass. Court: pointed, enclosed toe/heel, no strap, slip-on,
270 g. Slingback: pointed, enclosed toe/open heel, rear strap and adjustable buckle,
250 g. Dress sandal: almond, open toe/heel, ankle strap and adjustable buckle,
290 g. Open heel does not mean absent retention. A rear strap is not an ankle strap.

Nominal heel-height choices are 40/50/70/90 mm for courts, 50/70/90 for slingbacks,
40/50/63.5/70 for sandals. These broader unobserved heights are authored scope,
not source-calibrated frequencies. Block/slim geometry is a separate soft style-
conditioned choice; all styles can take either. Geometry does not prove stability.
Reference mass has uniform ±20 g noise and a 10 g authored block-heel increment;
its basis is one EU40 shoe, not a pair or a manufacturer-normalized measurement.

Upper choices are smooth leather, suede leather and synthetic. Rubber versus
thermoplastic rubber outsole is declared separately. These are synthetic factory
facts, not extrapolated missing fields of the named real products. Cushioning
can be minimal/modest without a quantified footbed thickness. Waterproof test
rating remains unknown. Heel-core material, heel cap, lining/insole, platform,
contact area, stiffness, foot geometry and certified grip are deferred.

Positioning and execution stay independent. Reference-price bands overlap at
SEK 400–1100, 1000–2000 and 1800–3500. Canonical adult nominal size/colour/width
manufacture is retained; half sizes, extra-wide and conversions remain deferred.
Style/closure/geometry changes are another model in this recipe. Retailer listing,
stock and selector/page grouping are later stages, not manufactured attributes.

## Text, applicable customer evidence and proposed tasks

Descriptions state style, toe/heel exposure, actual strap position/closure, upper,
outsole, cushioning, nominal heel geometry/height and single-shoe reference mass.
Proposed predicates can request maximum heel height, heel geometry or toe exposure;
they do not register tasks or imply that those facts guarantee comfort/safety.

General review meanings cover an evening occasion's comfort, forefoot fit, whether
the foot stays in place during an evening occasion, and purchase value. Ankle-strap
pressure is eligible only for the ankle-strap bundle. This applicability is selected
from the model before topics or wording, never inferred from the wording afterward.
Neither rear straps nor court shoes receive ankle-strap reports. Pressure is not
rubbing/blisters, foot movement is not a fall/broken buckle, and evening comfort
does not certify work shifts or long city walks. Customer meanings are authored;
no usable firsthand reviews were extracted from these examples.

Review subject is an exact manufactured SKU. Shared 75% silent default, star
concentration1.5, protected value frequency and modest fixed-satisfaction price
adjustment remain intact. Test the unchanged fixed seeds 0–19, all three style
compatibility branches and wrong-style rejection of ankle-strap wording. Keep
generic manufacture/grouping checks separate from human semantic acceptance.
Consumer adoption remains isolated authoring preview, with no old host changes.
