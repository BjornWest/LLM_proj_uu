## Formula and declared nutrition

Species cat, dry kibble presentation. Target kitten/adult/senior weights1/5/2:
labels respectively from weaning to12months, ages1–7years, ages11+years. These
are this recipe's declarations, not universal age boundaries or proof of an
individual cat's suitability. A life-stage filter uses the declared category;
age conversions and feeding-dose calculation are deferred.

Formula has a named dried chicken protein or dried salmon ingredient, weights3/1.
Its declared ingredient share is15/20/25% for chicken or12/18/22% for salmon,
equally. Rice, maize, animal fat, vegetable fibre and minerals are also named in
a **partial, unordered** composition disclosure. Other animal ingredients are
not excluded; no grain-free, single-protein, allergen-free or complete ingredient
partition follows. Formula changes are different models, not flavour selectors
that silently change an exact SKU.

Analytical label bundles, equally within target: kitten protein38/41%, fat18/20%,
crude fibre2/2.5%, ash7.5/8%; adult27/32%,13/15%,4/3%,7.7/7%; senior31.6/34%,
18.8/16%,1.1/2%,4.9/6%. Bundles are tunable, not a validated nutrient adequacy
profile. The synthetic label explicitly uses percent of food as sold; moisture,
calorific energy and analysis protocol remain unknown. Do not compute dry-matter
ratios or residual carbohydrate. Ingredient percentage is never added to the
analytical panel. An optional added-taurine declaration is700 or1000mg/kg, or
unknown, weights1/1/2; this additive dose is not an assayed total taurine content.

Dietary-role declaration is complete5/unspecified1. Unknown completeness is not
complementary and cannot pass a known-complete predicate. The complete label is
not independent validation against FEDIAF/AAFCO or an individual feeding plan.
Bag closure is tear-open non-reclosable or resealable zipper, weights1/2;
the actual represented closure controls applicable review wording. Net bag mass,
cat body weight and daily feeding grams are distinct; no dose table is supplied.

## Original owner-observed experiences

Concentration1.5,75% silent ratings and the accepted price-dependent value policy,
normalized to the tested pack's price range. Genuine non-value meanings: the owner
observed their target-stage cat eat or leave the food during ordinary meals in its
usual bowl; ease/difficulty pouring dry food into that bowl; and whether the owner
liked/disliked the smell on opening the bag in the usual feeding area. These are
owner observations/preferences, not an animal speaking or evidence of nutrition,
health effects or universal palatability. The target-stage context is retained.
Zipper opening/reclosing ease applies only to represented resealable bags;
tear-open bags never claim a zipper. Every review binds the exact formula/pack SKU.
