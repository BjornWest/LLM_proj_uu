## Authored recipe and dependencies

The apparel base supplies nominal alpha sizes, colours and sparse canonical
colour/size manufacture. Joint insulation assemblies: down in sewn baffles
(weight3; base garment centre380g) or polyester synthetic insulation retained by
quilting (weight3; centre320g). Shell fibre nylon/polyester2:1 is sampled separately;
lining polyester fixed. Both have dedicated fill, no membrane in this bounded
recipe, two zipped hand pockets and a full front zip. Fill species/blend,
component fibre percentages, seam treatment and quantitative waterproof/thermal
results are unknown. A quilt or baffle does not establish a waterproof barrier.

Down branch: authored declared fill power700/800cuin/oz equally, protocol and
conditioning unknown; total fill100/150/200g equally, for one nominalM reference
jacket. Hydrophobic-down treatment yes/no3:2. Synthetic branch: declared insulation
areal mass60/100g/m² equally; total fill grams unknown because no area/distribution
calculation is represented. Down-only fields are inapplicable to synthetic fill,
and synthetic areal mass is inapplicable to down. No threshold treats these units
as interchangeable. Numeric values are authored declarations, not measured thermal
performance or a promise of comparable laboratory protocols.

Hood absent/fixed/adjustable1:2:3; internal pocket usable as storage yes/no2:1.
Shell DWR present/absent3:1 independently; DWR is separate from hydrophobic down
and is not waterproofing. Cut slim/regular/relaxed1:5:2. Garment reference mass:
assembly centre+30g when a hood exists+10g when the storage pocket exists+integer
noise±25g, explicitly one nominalM garment. No calculation silently converts
synthetic g/m² into total fill or computes a size-specific mass. Price bands
SEK700–1500,1400–2800,2500–5000, round10. All weights/ranges and feature-mass
increments are tunable author judgment. No stock or destination input.

## Public facts and customer meanings

Show actual shell/lining/insulation/distribution, applicable fill quantities with
units and basis, hood/storage/DWR/down-treatment facts and unknown measurements.
No source mass normalized toM, numeric pack volume or safe temperature is added.
Shared concentration1.5,75% silent and modest price-dependent value policy;
canonical exact tested SKU. Generic meanings: perceived sufficient warmth during
a short cold dry walk over a light midlayer with front closed; arm movement in the
tested size over that midlayer; front-zip operation; value. Conditional hood
adjustment requires an adjustable hood. Packing ease requires the represented
internal storage pocket and means packing this jacket into that pocket; it does
not imply low packed volume or long-term loft retention.

## Acceptance and limits

Retain seeds0–19, both insulation assemblies, all hood/storage combinations and
both treatment states on down; verify quantities, inapplicable/unknown status,
reference-mass arithmetic and conditional review wording. Fill-kind, minimum
declared down fill power and maximum whole-garment mass predicates are proposals
only, not registered tasks or production filters. Numeric down predicate first
guards metric applicability explicitly, then requires down before comparison. A
kind-only Boolean guard is insufficient under strict unresolved-field validation.

Deferred: thermal physics, water/wind tests, insulation thickness/area and zoned
fill, actual feather/down ratios and species, SKU-specific masses/prices, body/cut
size charts, moisture/loft/care history and hybrid assemblies. Source examples and
synthetic opinions do not determine satisfaction or warmth from price/fill power.
