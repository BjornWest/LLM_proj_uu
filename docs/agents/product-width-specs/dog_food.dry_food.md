# Dry dog food — declared targets, formula panels and exact bags

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Concept `dog_food.dry_food`; family
`dog_food`. Bagged dry kibble, ordinary owner-observed meals. No clinical diet,
pregnancy/lactation target, individualized ration or medical outcome is implied.

## Primary examples and bounded judgment

Research in `.local/product-width/research/dry_dog_food.md` inspected
[Royal Canin Mini Adult](https://pro.royalcanin.co.uk/products/dog/mini-adult/),
[Hill's Medium Puppy Chicken](https://www.hillspet.co.uk/dog-food/science-plan-puppy-medium-chicken-dry)
and [Purina Large Robust Adult Chicken](https://www.purina.co.uk/dog/dog-food/product-proplan-adult-large-robust-everyday-chicken).
Their target/age/weight declarations differ; a feeding-table row or bag mass is
not a breed-size boundary. The sources support these distinctions, not sampling
frequencies or independent nutritional validation. Cat-food formulas and age
boundaries are not inherited.

## Target, composition and analytical declarations

One joint target recipe has small adult / medium puppy / large adult weights3/2/3.
Its model facts respectively carry small/medium/large breed-size categories,
adult/puppy/adult life stages and the literal age labels over10months / until12months /
onset18–24months through7years. The large label deliberately does not turn its
onset range into one exact month. Body-weight declarations are1–10kg for small,
numerical range unspecified for medium, and above25kg for large. These labels do
not calculate an individual dog's suitability; proposed filters use only declared
stage/size categories. Numerical age/weight matching is deferred, not approximated
from these strings. Puppy pregnancy/lactation use is explicitly outside this recipe.

Each formula names chicken, cereals, animal fat and minerals in a partial unordered
composition; other animal ingredients are not excluded. The named chicken share
is19% for the medium/large recipes and unknown for small. No nested poultry total,
single-protein/allergen-free/grain-free conclusion or complete composition is
manufactured. Formula/target changes identify different models, not pack selectors.

The analytical panel is known/unknown weights2/1. Known as-sold protein/fat/crude
fibre/crude ash percentages are28/16/2.5/7 for small (explicit authored bundle),
27.4/17.9/1.7/7.7 for medium and27/12/2.5/7.5 for large. Their partial sum must not
exceed100%; unknown panels retain unknown numeric values and render an explicit
unspecified disclosure. Moisture and test protocol are unknown. No dry-matter
ratio, residual carbohydrate or adequacy judgement follows.

Large models separately declare368kcal/100g energy and analytical taurine0.1%,
even when the four-field panel is undisclosed; these are independent label
declarations, not values inferred from it. Energy and analytical taurine are
unknown for the other targets. Added L-carnitine is50mg/kg or unknown, weights1/2,
in a separate nutritional-additive field. It is never a total finished-food assay
or the source's analytical L-carnitine percentage. Calorie method, total
L-carnitine assay and individual suitability remain unknown.

Dietary role is complete/unspecified weights5/1. Unknown is not complementary or
known complete; the label does not establish FEDIAF/AAFCO adequacy. Bag closure is
tear-open non-reclosable/resealable zipper1/2, an authored mechanism. Kibble
dimensions, piece count and daily feeding grams remain unknown; an unscaled image
or a breed-size target cannot supply them.

## Dog-specific bag manufacture and reference prices

A new supporting module supplies neutral positioning/execution and dog dry-bag
manufacture only. Sparse nonempty menus select1/3/8/14kg bags with weights3/4/3/2
and menu-size weights35/40/20/5. These extra sizes are authored possibilities,
not a manufacturer's full assortment. Pack mass is net food mass, never dog weight.
Canonical SKU fields are identity/parent, net mass in grams, dimensionless price
coefficient and exact reference price. Only mass is a retailer grouping axis;
all other canonical facts survive sparse, empty and sold-out grouping.

The synthetic1kg anchor is40–90SEK budget,80–150 mainstream and140–260 premium.
Pack coefficients1/2.7/6.4/10.5 make exact bag reference prices. They are tunable
authored associations, not quotes, stock or a feeding-cost claim. Reviews use the
selected bag's price and scale both40/260SEK normalization bounds by its coefficient;
small bags are not automatically rewarded as better value.

## Owner evidence and acceptance

Reuse concentration1.5,75% silent ratings and the accepted price-dependent value
policy. Non-value meanings: owner observed their declared size/stage dog eat or
leave the food at ordinary meals in its usual bowl; ease/difficulty pouring dry
food from the tested bag into that bowl; and owner smell preference on opening
that bag in the usual feeding area. Zipper opening/reclosing applies only to the
represented zipper. Both polarity/wording variants retain target context and
exact formula/bag SKU. No dog testimony, digestive/coat/dental/joint/growth/weight
improvements, freshness guarantee or measured feeding dose is asserted.

Retain seeds0–19 and force all three targets, known/unknown analytical panels,
role/additive states, both closures and all four bags. Render every applicable
catalog pool and reject inactive ones, including unknown target-body-weight,
ingredient, analysis, energy and taurine disclosures. Independently verify pack
arithmetic/value normalization, canonical grouping and actual owner context.
Declared stage, breed size and known-complete filters remain proposals. No shared
compiler, current host, production sampler/task/grader or prior acceptance changes.

Deferred: more stages/size targets, physiological/clinical diets, complete ordered
formulas, ingredient substitutions, numerical animal matching, daily doses,
piece geometry, batch/expiry, nutrition validation and empirical calibration.
