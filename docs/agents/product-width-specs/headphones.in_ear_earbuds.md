# In-ear earbuds — bounded executable authoring recipe

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Concept
`headphones.in_ear_earbuds`; family `headphones`. Tip-based ear-canal pairs with
passive analogue or true-wireless Bluetooth connections. Conventional tipless buds,
USB-audio buds, neckbands and sport ear hooks are outside this first recipe.

## Sources and reused meaning

Research `.local/product-width/research/in_ear_earbuds.md` uses

The latter is a boundary example: USB-C can carry audio, unlike our explicitly
charging-only case. No universal negative follows from source silence. Tip menus
are supplied accessories, not separately manufactured earbud size SKUs. Sony's
bud/case masses and music contexts support keeping component bases separate.
Examples establish plausible distinctions, not the probabilities below.

Reuse colour-only electronics manufacture and the shared rating/value policy.
No ear-cup/clamp, adult clothing, shoe or headband geometry is inherited. Familiar
playlist vocal clarity and bass preference remain subjective contexts; ear fit
requires its own meaning and an actual chosen supplied tip.

## Facts, conditional draws and scope

Joint connection choice passive analogue2 / true wireless3. Passive has a stereo
3.5mm source plug, no charging case, no electronics battery and no included mic or
inline control in this recipe. Its analogue cable may be captive/detachable equally.
True wireless has Bluetooth5.2/5.3, rechargeable buds, an included charging case and
a Bluetooth call mic; no analogue connection. The case charges via USB-C, not USB
audio. Optional compatible Qi case charging yes/no1:2; no charging pad assumed
included. Earbud charging is through case contacts, not its USB-C socket.

Both have closed dynamic ear-canal design with7/9/11mm drivers, equally weighted.
Diameter does not determine sound quality. One supplied tip material silicone/foam
3:1 with S/M/L sizes; a reviewer chooses one of those labels, not a bodily-size
conversion. The pair's manufactured variants remain colour only. No ear hooks or
fins in this recipe; ear anatomy, seal pressure and source-exhaustive accessories
are not inferred.

ANC only wireless, yes/no2:3. Ambient passthrough sampled separately yes/no1:1
within wireless; neither proves passive isolation or quantified attenuation.
Wireless playback controls are buttons/touch equally; passive has no represented
playback controls. Control gestures, volume support, wired microphone accessories,
codecs/apps/firmware/host matrices remain unspecified. Driver/fit menus and these
negative facts are bounded choices, not general properties of earbuds.

Declared wireless music maximum per bud charge with ANC off4/6/8h; with ANC on it
is off maximum minus1h. Cumulative case-assisted ANC-off music declaration equals
four times the per-charge maximum, explicitly requiring recharge interruptions;
it is not uninterrupted playback or cell energy. Bud charging in case1/1.5h and
case USB charging2/3h separately declared. Test protocols and call runtime unknown.
Applicable nominal bud and case battery energies are independently unspecified;
no mAh, charger voltage or hours are converted to Wh. Every battery/charging/case
quantity is not applicable for passive analogue models, not zero or unknown.

Pair mass including fitted M tips but excluding cable/case: passive centre12g,
wireless centre10g, integer noise±2g. Wireless case excluding buds centre40/55g
with noise±3g. Do not present pair mass as per bud, or include case in bud mass.
IP protection declarations, measured isolation/ANC attenuation and latency are
unknown; no waterproof/exposure protection inferred. Model reference price bands
SEK150–500,450–1400,1200–3500, round10; no retailer stock/quote/colour surcharges.

## Reviews and evidence

Concentration1.5,75% silent ratings and modest price/value association are retained.
Generic original meanings: wearing comfort after30min seated indoors using the
chosen supplied tip size; perceived vocal clarity and bass preference on a familiar
playlist at usual comfortable level; fitting/removing supplied tips; value. Tip
handling is not a claim of fit/retention or all-day comfort. All opinions bind the
exact purchased colour pair SKU; left/right earbuds and tips are not new SKU IDs.

Wireless-only topics: Bluetooth pairing with a compatible phone, operating actual
earbud playback buttons/touch controls, and inserting both buds into the matching
charging-case slots. The last concerns handling, not achieved charging/runtime.
Detachable-cable handling applies only to a represented detachable analogue cable.
Train background-noise reduction opinions require actual ANC enabled and retain
sufficient/insufficient subjective wording. No call-quality, water/sport safety,
uninterrupted case-total lifetime or charging-success testimony is inferred.
