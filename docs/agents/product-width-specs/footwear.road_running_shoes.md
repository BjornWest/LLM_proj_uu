# Road-running shoes — domain specification

Status: domain accepted after prose, DSL, fixed-seed tests and independent review, 2026-10-01. Registry concept `footwear.road_running_shoes`.
Scope: enclosed adult running footwear marketed primarily for paved-road running;
ordinary walking remains possible, but no race-time or injury-prevention guarantee.

Primary-source observations are retained in
`.local/product-width/research/road_running_shoes.md`. They support mesh/knit foam
running designs, declared drops and varying widths; no published weight without a
reference-size basis is treated as our EU40 measurement. Rubber outsole coverage
with exposed foam is realistic but only an aggregate rubber-outsole description
is represented in this bounded package; detailed coverage percentage is deferred.
Examples: [ASICS GEL-CUMULUS 28](https://www.asics.com/nz/en-nz/gel-cumulus-28-mens-1011c143-403),
[ASICS GEL-NIMBUS 27](https://www.asics.com/nz/en-nz/gel-nimbus-27-mens-1011b958-001)
and [Saucony Endorphin Speed 5 Wide](https://www.saucony.com/en/endorphin-speed-5-wide/60707M.html?dwvar_60707M_color=S21008-01).
The plated Saucony example demonstrates a real subtype deliberately deferred here;
it does not imply every fast-session shoe requires a plate.

## Meaningful distinctions and dependencies

Use the accepted categorical session target: short/fast sessions, everyday training,
or long-distance sessions. This is private intended-workload emphasis, separate
from positioning and independent execution. It softly weights three coherent
construction choices: lighter moderate foam cushioning, everyday balanced foam, or
more substantial foam cushioning. Target is not a measured speed/comfort outcome;
all three constructions remain possible for every target.

Jointly choose the sole/cushioning base and EU40 single-shoe reference mass, then
sample bounded mass noise. Upper is engineered mesh or knit with overlays; outsole
is rubber with shallow/moderate road-oriented tread. Lacing is the bounded recipe
closure. No carbon plate or stability/motion-control subsystem is claimed in this
first package; those require additional facts if introduced. Waterproofing remains
unknown. Heel-to-toe drop is a declared nominal millimetre value sampled independently
within a modest finite grid; it is not shoe heel height or proof of an individual's
appropriate gait. Cushioning softness and drop do not determine execution.

Positioning weights commercial reference bands. All numeric distributions, grids
and profile associations are authored tunable heuristics, not estimates from a few
manufacturer pages. Mass basis is synthetic and explicitly labelled; extracted real
weights without a reference-size qualifier must not be copied as our basis.

## Manufactured choices and public evidence

Reuse nominal adult size/colour/width manufacture and sparse canonical SKU tuples.
Construction/foam/drop changes create a different model here. Retailers choose page
partition versus selector roles and list canonical subsets before stock; no complete
size-width-colour grid or shared fit across brands is assumed. Extra-wide widths,
half sizes and brand conversion charts remain explicit first-wave deferrals.

Descriptions/specifications disclose upper, sole, cushioning, drop and labelled mass.
Review meaning banks cover value, ordinary road-run comfort, forefoot fit,
outsole wear over repeated road runs, and upper ventilation while road running.
Exact canonical tested SKU/context/polarity are retained. Weather protection, race
performance, long-run distance and medical benefit are not inferred from these
topics. Rating concentration 1.5 and the protected value policy are reused, with
silent ratings and variable statement count/order. Wording variants preserve the
same event and duration, rather than introducing new facts during paraphrasing.

Export proposed catalog predicates for cushioning and a maximum nominal drop;
clearly mark them unregistered with task/filter consumers. Size/width/colour tuple
resolution and context-qualified review thresholds remain downstream bindings.

## Acceptance

Fixed seeds 0–19, repeated determinism, construction/mass coherence, numeric units,
manufactured identity/sparse grouping and exact review-subject tests. Check stated
session target remains a soft weight, not a hard conditional product identity.
Primary examples and their precise design observations are recorded separately.
No hidden retries, no seed search, no model episodes or published-host replacement.

## Review-policy correction (new acceptance revision)

Default ratings are 75% silent; written lengths retain 4:3:1 weights. Value favourability follows overall stars with independent clipped normal noise (spread 0.22), adjusted by `-0.2 * (relative_price - 0.5)`. Relative price uses the concept's full reference-price band in SEK; the synthetic recorded purchase price is initialized from model reference, not a live retailer quote. This is the agreed pilot calibration, not a claim cheap products have higher quality. Price changes value sentiment only; stars/topics remain separate. Earlier cohorts and source versions are preserved under `.local/product-width/history/review-defaults-v1/`.
