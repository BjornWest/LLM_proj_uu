# Shell phone cases — exact-device constructions and owner handling

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Concept `phone_cases.shell_cases`, family
`phone_cases`, electronics store theme. Removable protective shells for one explicitly
declared device model. No folio cover/card pocket, universal fit or screen-size-based
compatibility. Additive authoring only; runtime phone/case matching is not adopted.

## Target-device fixture and constructions

Sample exactly one of two separately named authoring device IDs, compact/mid3:2.
The compact UK fixture has a declared USB-C port, ordinary power/volume controls
and a rear-camera area; the mid UK fixture has the same types of controls with
different authored placement. Exact case compatibility is a declaration attached
to that ID; it is **not derived from similar dimensions or screen diagonal**.
The IDs do not claim to be real iPhone/Pixel models or currently generated phone
product IDs. Production matching and CAD geometry remain deferred; no hidden
cross-package substitution establishes compatibility.

Three joint constructions, baseline weights6:3:2, with positioning associations
budget8:2:1, mainstream5:4:2, premium2:4:5. These are tunable authored correlations,
not hard rules or market probabilities:

| Assembly | Explicit authored construction/operation | Magnetic/stand state |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Flexible wraparound | TPU shell, textured exterior, raised screen/camera edges with numerical lip height unknown; covered power/volume buttons; open USB-C access | Built-in magnets absent and case stand absent as explicit authored choices, not conclusions from Liquid Air silence; compatible wireless charging declared, achieved charging unknown |
| Hybrid stand | PC back and TPU bumper as explicit authored component allocation; raised screen/camera edges, covered power/volume buttons and open USB-C access | Built-in magnets present; compatible magnetic/wireless route declared, conditional on matching phone/charger and stand fully folded; integrated flush-folding stand supports authored landscape flat-desk use |
| Rugged layers | PC inner shell plus TPE exterior, textured surface, raised edges, covered power/volume buttons, hinged USB-C port cover and strap anchors | Built-in magnets present; compatible magnetic/wireless route declared with cover/charger clearance unassessed; case-body stand unknown, not inferred from a separately available holster |

Covered side-button operation, case attachment architecture and the exact component
allocation are recipe facts, including where source pages do not specify them.
Raised edges do not establish scratch-free surfaces, face-down clearance for every
screen protector or successful drop protection. Rugged port cover does not confer
IP/waterproof protection. Layer proportions, quantitative rigidity, lip dimensions,
impact height/surface/pass protocol, actual drop survival and magnetic retention
strength remain unknown. Source 7× military-standard language is not copied as a
sevenfold survival probability or certification. Materials are component-specific;
the recipe does not generate percentages or pretend a mixed case is pure TPU.

One case unit per SKU. Manufactured colour subsets black/blue/clear1–3, with
nonempty counts1/2/3 weights3:4:2. Rugged allows black/blue only and counts1/2
weights3:4; clear is outside that authored menu, not physically impossible for every
TPE case. Clear is a cosmetic transparent-style label in flexible/hybrid fixtures,
not a guarantee of optical properties, anti-yellowing duration or unchanged material.
No colour changes the declared target fit, hardware, reference price or protection.
Canonical SKU records preserve exact device ID, colour and model reference price.
Device and colour may be offered on different retailer pages, but only colour is
the current model's grouping axis. Sparse carry, sold-out and empty offerings remain
retailer states; no missing colour gets manufactured to satisfy a request.

## Compatibility, included items and disclosure

Only a compatible route is declared, not a universally successful charge or a case
power output. The phone must itself support the represented route; charger/accessory
clearance, exact compatible-device regional revisions and charging wattage remain
unknown. Cases do not upgrade the phone's battery or create wireless capability.
Hybrid stand must be folded for its declared route. Built-in magnet presence is a
hardware fact, not equivalent to Qi/MagSafe performance or certified compatibility.
No review infers successful charging from either declaration.

Only the shell is included as an explicit authored package choice. A charger,
separate holster, strap or screen protector is not included in this recipe; the
source offers/omissions do not establish these exclusions without that authorship.
Rugged strap anchors do not imply a supplied strap. No accessory holster is exposed
as a case-body stand. Screen-protector fit and case/device dimensional tolerances
remain unknown. Unknown stand/protection/clearance must have guarded disclosures,
never false or a zero lifetime. Case mass/packaging mass remain unknown rather than
mixing source ounce figures across device versions or silently converting them.

## Reference price, opinions and proposed requests

Model reference bands SEK70–180/150–400/300–900 by independent positioning,
rounded5; assembly probabilities share the positioning parent, while exact price
still varies within the band. These are authored synthetic prices, not current
vendor quotes. No colour surcharge, purchase quantity or accessory bundle. Reviews
use the selected exact colour/device SKU price with global70–900 bounds and the
accepted modest cheap-at-fixed-satisfaction value boost; concentration1.5 and75%
silent ratings remain unchanged. Private execution is not protection evidence.

Original first-hand topics keep the exact supported fixture device and tested SKU:
seating/removing this case on that device; dry-hand grip during ordinary use;
putting the cased device into and taking it out of the owner's usual pocket;
operating its actually covered side power button; attaching the owner's usual
USB-C plug, with a represented port cover open; and value. No universal pocket/hand
fit, measured pressing force, successful charge or scratch/drop result is inferred.

Hybrid-only stand topic: unfolding/folding the integrated stand and using the cased
device in landscape on a flat desk. Rugged-only port-cover topic: opening/closing
its actual hinged cover. Both polarities retain the same action/device/condition;
unknown stand never becomes an eligible stand topic. The fixed claims do not dictate
customer polarity. Do not include generic phone camera/battery/app-quality opinions.

Request/filter proposals: exact supported-device ID, component material, built-in
magnet presence and integrated stand presence with unknown-state guards. A request
for no stand must not be satisfied by unknown stand. Fit means declared identity,
not a numerical-clearance inference. Task/oracle/rendering integration stays proposed.
