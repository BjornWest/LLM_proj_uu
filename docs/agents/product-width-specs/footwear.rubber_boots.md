# Rubber boots — bounded executable authoring recipe

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Concept `footwear.rubber_boots`;
family `footwear`. Additive authored scope; no production consumer registration.

## Scope and primary examples

Rubber-upper wet-weather boots with pull-on entry. Bounded research in
`.local/product-width/research/rubber_boots.md` compares
[Hunter Original Tall](https://hunterboots.com/products/womens-original-tall-rain-boots-in-black-wft1000rma-hbl01)
and [AIGLE Parcours2 ISO](https://www.aigle.com/uk/en/p/parcours-2-iso-3246576019283.html).
Hunter distinguishes pull-on entry from a shaft buckle; AIGLE distinguishes a
calf-adjusting gusset from neoprene lining/insulation. Unstated dimensions, reference
sizes, outsole formulations and footbed removability are not invented. The recipe
below is a bounded plausibility proposal, not a copied bill of materials.

## Sampling dependencies

Positioning/execution and nominal adult EU size/colour/width manufacture reuse the
neutral footwear module. Rubber upper and rubber outsole are authored fixed scope
facts; exact rubber formulation and proportion remain unspecified. Two joint lining
bundles: textile lining/no dedicated insulation declared (weight4, mass centre700g),
or neoprene lining/neoprene insulation (weight1,950g). Neither has a removable inner
boot. Unknown footbed composition and removability remain explicit; sole cushioning
is not used to infer those properties.

Mid/tall shaft weights2:3; tall adds60g. Uniform integer mass noise±30g is one
synthetic EU40 boot, not a source pair conversion. Calf adjustment is sampled
separately: none3, shaft buckle strap2, gusset adjuster with unspecified hardware1.
Each remains possible for either lining bundle and shaft height. Entry remains
pull-on; an adjustment strap is not a zipper/lace entry closure. Calf dimensions and
adjustment range are deferred. Waterproof construction declaration yes:no is5:1;
no declaration is not evidence of permeability. Quantitative waterproof/temperature
ratings are unknown. No protective or slip-resistance certification is asserted.

Synthetic SEK reference-price bands: budget350–800, mainstream700–1400,
premium1200–2400, round10. All weights, prices and mass centres/increments are
tunable authored choices. SKU-dependent mass/price, measured calf geometry,
half-size conversions and extra-wide rules are deferred. Sparse manufactured
tuples remain separate from downstream listing and stock availability.

## Public description and reviews

Expose pull-on entry, shaft, components, insulation, inner-boot identity, calf
adjuster, declaration/unknown ratings, unknown footbed details and mass/basis.
Language describes these facts without treating a rubber upper as an unlimited
waterproof guarantee. Insulated rubber boots may overlap the winter-boot concept;
purpose and construction are separate axes rather than universal exclusions.

Shared exact canonical tested SKU, concentration1.5 stars, 75% silent ratings,
variable written topics and accepted price-dependent value policy. Original
opinions cover perceived foot dryness during a short rainy walk with water below
the shaft; forefoot room with ordinary socks in the tested size; ease of putting
the boots on; and calf-adjuster ease only when a represented adjuster exists.
Dry/wet feet do not identify seam failure, perspiration or waterproof certification.
Entry difficulty is not hardware failure. Adjustment ease is not a claim that the
boot fits every calf. No invented fall, injury or ice-grip outcome.

## Acceptance and consumer limits

Retain fixed seeds0–19; test both lining/insulation bundles, all adjuster/shaft
branches, mass arithmetic, pull-on identity, source-bound content, unknown footbed
and ratings, conditional topic selection and forced inapplicable wording rejection.
Manufacture/grouping preserve sparse SKU tuples and sold-out/no-match examples.
Calf-adjuster, lining and declaration predicates are exported proposals; missing
request values reject. No registered tasks, filter consumer, website or grader is
claimed. Short/ankle styles, removable liners, non-rubber polymers, occupational
capabilities and physical outcome models remain deferred.
