## Public content and exact-SKU experience

Describe actual main insulation, applicable fill metrics/bases, shell/membrane/DWR,
unknown ratings, explicit centre-back length basis, actual hood/trim/zip/flap and
hand pockets. Keep material/component declarations separate from experienced warmth.
Concentration1.5,75% silent ratings, modest shared price/value policy. Generic
experiences: warmth sufficiency during a short cold dry walk over a light midlayer
with front closed; walking/leg movement comfort in tested size over that midlayer
with front closed; front entry-zip operation; value. Walking comfort is not length,
universal wearer coverage or objectively unrestricted stride. Conditional topics:
hood adjustment only adjustable hood; operating lower slider to open front from
below only two-way zip; removing/replacing faux trim only removable faux trim.
Each polarity/paraphrase preserves operation/context, without inferred hardware
failure, rain dryness, temperature guarantee or removable-hood claim.

## Acceptance and deferrals

Retain seeds0–19; force insulation, hood, trim, membrane/publication and zip/flap
branches. Check units/statuses, collar/hem length basis, garment arithmetic and
conditional controls. Minimum declared reference length, actual two-way function
and minimum declared down power have proposed-only predicates with explicit
metric applicability guard; missing requests reject. No production renderer,
task/filter registration or outcome grader adoption.

Deferred: numerical body dimensions, anatomical coverage, wearer height,
size/gender conversion, zoned fill, real feather/down ratios/species, trim removal
geometry, pocket layouts, detachable hoods, MVTR/RET, garment water testing,
thermal/care physics and actual SKU-specific masses. Material/faux trim declarations
do not establish animal-free manufacture or tested laboratory comparability.
