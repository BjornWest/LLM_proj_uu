# Open-ear/sport headphones — bounded executable authoring recipe

Status: domain accepted in shared DSL, 2026-10-01; isolated authoring preview only. Concept
`headphones.open_ear_sport_headphones`; family `headphones`. Open ear canal without
sealing tips: bone-conduction rear-band assemblies and independent air-conduction
hook/cuff pairs. Sport is a represented use context, not a safety guarantee.

charging and complete-device versus pair masses are independent meanings, even
where this first recipe joins particular choices. Do not copy mixed model/series
panels, ambiguous sizing charts or source accessory/mass bases. Manufacturer
awareness, leakage, fit and comfort prose is not measured customer evidence.

Reuse colour-only electronics manufacture and ratings/value policy. Fixed nominal
adult rear-band configuration is a separate model construction; smaller bands and
charging-hardware variants are deferred, not a forced Cartesian colour/size menu.
Neither ear tips, ear-cup clamp nor analog cable handling is inherited.

## Dependencies and declarations

Joint assembly menu: bone-conduction connected rear-band3, air-conduction independent
ear hooks3, air-conduction independent cuffs2. These are bounded authored templates,
not physical prohibitions against other combinations. All have Bluetooth5.2/5.3,
rechargeable playback, buttons and a Bluetooth call microphone. ANC, analogue and
USB audio explicitly absent in this recipe. A call mic does not imply ambient ANC.
Acoustic measurement, driver dimensions, app/codec/host compatibility and ambient
audibility guarantees are unspecified.

Rear-band device charges directly through magnetic contacts/USB-C equally; its
charger route is not an audio connector. Independent pairs charge through matching
case contacts; case USB-C charges the case. Compatible Qi case charging yes/no1:3.
No case or case quantities apply to the rear-band device. Nominal wearable cell
energy is unknown with basis one connected device versus each independent bud;
case energy separately unknown where a case exists. Never derive Wh from hours or
charger voltage. Battery protocols and call runtime remain unspecified.

Music maximum per charge8/10/12h for rear band,6/8/10h for independent pairs. Their
case-assisted total is three times the per-charge declaration, explicitly requiring
recharge interruptions. Wearable empty-charge declaration1/1.5h, basis connected
device directly versus buds inside matching case; case USB empty-charge2/3h. These
menus/ratios are synthetic declarations, not physics or experiential guarantees.

Mass excluding cable/charger/case: one connected device centre28g±3g; independent
hook pair centre20g±2g; cuff pair centre12g±2g. Do not call pair mass per bud.
Independent case excluding buds centre40/55g±3g. Local contact material silicone
does not describe the complete device's material composition. Published wearable
IP label may be unspecified/IPX4/IP55/IP67, weights2/4/3/1; only wearable units,
not charging case or hardware. No swimming, hazard-awareness or exposure protection
claim follows. Reference SEK bands400–1000,900–2000,1800–3500, round10; manufacture
has no retailer stock, quotes or colour premiums.

## Customer meanings

Concentration1.5,75% silent ratings and the existing modest cheap-price value boost.
Generic original topics: wearing comfort30min seated indoors; bass preference on a
familiar playlist at usual comfortable level in a quiet room; staying in position
during a10min easy jog on a dry level route; Bluetooth pairing to a compatible
phone; operating actual playback buttons; value. Retention does not promise sport,
traffic or water safety, and a dislike is not evidence of product malfunction.

Case-slot handling applies only to independent pairs. Direct charging-connector
handling names represented magnetic contacts or USB-C, only on rear-band models.
Bone-contact vibration preference during familiar music at usual comfortable level
applies only to the represented bone-conduction assembly; it does not imply pain,
injury or air-driver pressure. All contexts/polarities bind exact canonical colour
SKU of the complete assembly/pair, not a new left/right unit. No ANC, ear-tip,
call-quality, case-total uninterrupted endurance or environmental-safety testimony.

## Acceptance and extensions

Retain seeds0–19; force all three assemblies, both direct connectors, both case
charging choices and every declaration state. Verify model/configuration versus
colour SKU, component applicability and quantity bases, mass ranges, hours/energy
separation, context-bound language and sparse/sold-out/empty listing grouping.
Proposed transmission/support/IP-label filters require supplied requests; production
filters/tasks/oracles are unchanged. A known IP label is not a protection-protocol
grader. Preserve every earlier cohort, package/base and host.

Deferred: regional bands/charging configurations, bone independent buds or air rear
bands, firmware/codec/app routing, precise ear/head/glasses geometry, clamping and
leakage/latency/attenuation tests, source-normalized masses, numerical cells,
certification protocols, single-bud operation, accessory inventory and empirical
calibration. These limitations do not turn unknown performance into false facts.
