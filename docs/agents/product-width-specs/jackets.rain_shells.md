unspecified-size jacket or fabric areal mass.

## Dependencies and manufactured identity

`_apparel_base.json` supplies positioning/execution, nominal adult alpha sizing,
colours and sparse manufactured colour/size-label tuples. No footwear facts.
Three joint laminate recipes, all authored weights: 2-layer polyester face/PU
membrane/separate mesh lining (weight2,350g); 2.5-layer nylon face/PU membrane/
protective inner print (weight4,300g); 3-layer nylon face/PU membrane/tricot backing
(weight3,420g). Layer count refers to laminate structure: the separate mesh lining
does not make the2-layer laminate3-layer. These generic component recipes are not
verified exact source bills of materials. No dedicated insulating fill is declared.

All seams taped is an explicit authored baseline, not inferred from layer count or
the two source pages. DWR yes:no4:1; front full zip fixed. Regular/relaxed cut3:1.
Hood joint draw: absent1, attached-adjustable6, detachable-adjustable2; no stowing or
helmet-compatibility claim. Underarm zip vents present:absent3:2. Mass is one
synthetic sizeM garment, bundle centre+25g if hood exists+15g if vents exist,
uniform integer noise±20g. Positioning price bands SEK400–1000,900–2000,1800–4000,
round10. No size-specific mass or SKU surcharge is inferred.

Two independent publication choices retain missing test data: declared hydrostatic
head present:absent2:1, then10,000/20,000mm weights1:1; declared MVTR present:absent
1:1, then10,000/20,000g/m²/24h weights1:1. Optional numeric declaration fields are applicable only when published. The
compiler represents an absent declaration as a `not_applicable` envelope, never
zero. This refers to the claim, not to physical waterproof/breathability capability,
which remains unknown. Test methods/conditioning
remain unspecified even when values are present. These are authored declarations,
not measurements taken from the synthetic fabric or comparable certifications.
MVTR is not hydrostatic head; a higher number cannot be used across metrics.

## Public content and customer meanings

Fact-bound descriptions expose laminate components/layer count, seam/DWR scope,
front entry, cut, hood/vent presence and mass basis. Separate conditional pools
publish numeric declarations with correct units, or say that the result was not
published. Do not fill unknown values from branded standards. Nominal labels do
not establish body fit, gender/cut equivalence or helmet compatibility.

Shared ratings concentration1.5,75% silent, accepted price-dependent value policy,
exact canonical tested SKU and variable written topics/order. Meanings: perceived
torso dryness on a short rainy walk with front closed; room over a light midlayer
in the tested size; front entry-zip operation ease; hood-adjustment ease only when an adjustable hood exists;
underarm-vent operation ease only when the vents exist; value. Dampness does not
identify leakage rather than sweat/condensation. Operating vents is not measured
MVTR or less clamminess. Adjustment/zip operation difficulty does not imply broken
hardware. The three generic non-value topics remain applicable even with no hood
and no vents, making the shared written-length distribution valid in that branch.

## Acceptance and explicit limits

Retain seeds0–19. Check laminate/backing coherence, numeric metric units and missing
values, mass increments, hood/vent applicability (including forced wrong-feature
wording), alphabetic canonical SKU manufacture, sparse retailer grouping and
source-bound formulation. Proposed predicates for hood/vents and minimum declared
head reject missing request values; unknown product head cannot satisfy a numeric
claim. No registered task, connected world, grader or production renderer.

Deferred: test protocols/conditioning, RET, measured leak/condensation response,
gender/body/cut conversion, pocket geometry, stowing/helmet fit, sleeve/cuff/hem
adjusters, care/wear effects and construction-dependent customer outcomes. Later
consumers must not compare unqualified manufacturer test figures as if measured
under a common protocol.
