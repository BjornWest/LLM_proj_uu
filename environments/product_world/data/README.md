# Offline Western Europe fixture

`europe_locations.json` contains 59 selected city anchors in SE, NO, DK, FI, IS,
GB, IE, NL, BE, LU, FR, DE, AT, CH, ES, PT and IT. This is an explicit project
sampling scope, not a claim about an official regional classification. It excludes
most towns, rural settlements and island territories. Selection is curated, not a
population-weighted or market-representative sample.

City names, WGS84 coordinates and timezone fields are adapted from **GeoNames**,
<https://www.geonames.org/>, under **CC BY 4.0**,
<https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>. The source is the cities15000
snapshot retrieved 2026-09-26, a 3,359,528-byte ZIP:
<https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/cities15000.zip>.
The JSON records the exact archive SHA-256 and original GeoNames IDs. Field format
and license: <https://download.geonames.org/export/dump/readme.txt>.

Changes: selected records, added stable city IDs, retained only required fields.
The source archive is retained locally at
`.local/product-world/geography/cities15000.zip`; it is not needed during generation.
The source's population estimates are deliberately not used. GeoNames supplies
coordinates, not the routes below.

The **64 corridor edges are authored synthetic routing assumptions**. They are not
OpenStreetMap data, surveyed roads or current carrier/ferry schedules. Distances and
times are coarse estimates from coordinates, detour factors, speed and handling
assumptions. Crossings are explicit bridge, bridge/tunnel, ferry or freight-only air
edges; they cannot turn into walking/ordinary-road shortcuts. The Manchester–Dublin
edge abstracts port access and a sea crossing, not a literal city-centre ferry route.
The Reykjavík–Copenhagen cargo edge models fictional freight connectivity, not a
verified commercial service. Ground passenger travel to Iceland is unmodelled.

Local home/branch/warehouse positions are fictional offsets of up to 4 km from city
anchors. They have **no actual address, street snapping, coastline check or verified
land parcel**. This fixture is suitable for internally consistent synthetic tasks;
use cached road-network routes plus valid endpoint snapping before claiming real
navigation fidelity.

Do not silently refresh a data snapshot: IDs, coordinates and route assumptions are
world-version inputs. No module reads the internet or this source archive at runtime.

## Branch area labels

`areas.py` adds a small curated name vocabulary for Uppsala, Malmö, Copenhagen and
Amsterdam. Primary references for names:

- [Uppsala's inner-city plan](https://www.uppsala.se/contentassets/27449eed7a2541c7b046f38bd7f3af8f/plan-for-innerstaden-20241119.pdf): Luthagen, Fålhagen, Kungsängen.
- [Malmö's area descriptions](https://malmo.se/Fakta-och-statistik/Statistik-for-Malmos-omraden/Vad-ar-delomraden-och-GeSO.html): Västra Hamnen, Möllevången, Sorgenfri.
- [Visit Copenhagen neighbourhood guide](https://www.visitcopenhagen.com/copenhagen/areas/neighborhoods/the-copenhagen-neighbourhood-guide): Vesterbro, Nørrebro, Østerbro, Christianshavn.
- [I amsterdam neighbourhood guide](https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/explore/neighbourhoods): De Pijp, Oud-West, Oost.

Relative anchors for these labels are authored approximations, not coordinates or
boundary geometry extracted from those sources. Branch coordinates receive bounded
jitter around the selected anchor, so names and positions share one sampling input.
Other cities, and additional locations after named areas are used, use clearly typed
synthetic centre/directional sectors. These are descriptive branch names, not asserted
official administrative districts. Named areas and generic sectors may overlap.
One retailer cannot repeat an area/name within a city; other retailers can share areas.
Area selection never raises the sampled branch count. No addresses, verified land
parcels or real businesses are generated by these labels.
