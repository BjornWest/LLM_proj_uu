2. Generate a world for this task
Regenerating stores keeps the displayed task and memories fixed. The task determines family coverage and any named-model identity; catalog facts, prices and stock are sampled separately. Unrelated departments can occur only in a labelled general marketplace.







Advanced store and catalog probabilities
Zero family weights exclude goods everywhere, so the selected scope must remain enabled. Generalists are capped at one and require at least three stores. Model reuse is conditional on existing compatible models; focused families also have one shared listing anchor.
Top-level controls override corresponding advanced fields. Other controls include generalist_probability, profile_weights and variant_carry_probability. Catalog fields include field_weights, axis_option_counts and variant_omission_probability.
Size profiles correlate model count, warehouse stock range, branch count and international delivery. Product specialization remains separate. Location probabilities: home_city_probability + home_country_probability ≤ 1; foreign_neighbour_probability biases foreign stores toward countries connected by ground/sea corridors; branch_stockout_probability controls physical stock independently. Merchant home overrides use store IDs and bundled city IDs, e.g. {"store-001":"copenhagen"}.
