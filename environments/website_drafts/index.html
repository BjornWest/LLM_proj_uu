What to try
  1. Use “Load example basket”, then Cart or Checkout, to compare those units immediately. This review control replaces only the current preview’s basket.
  2. Switch global conventions without changing the units. Labels, currency notation and option controls should stay consistent across the shop.
  3. Choose different product, cart and checkout units. Add Granvik Mela and Granvik Task Lamp, then inspect delivery together and in separate arrival groups.
  4. Choose another shoe size. Its mass and scope change together. EU 44 of Granvik Mela is unavailable; Granvik Ridge is an orderable supplier backorder.
  5. Use WELCOME10 with products totaling at least 500 SEK. Totals and free-delivery qualification update after changes.
  6. Save postcode 11122 in checkout, then revisit a product. Its estimate uses that explicitly saved address. Browser back/reload normally preserves the draft.
  7. Try home delivery, pickup boxes, staffed parcel points or shop collection. Each service row shows its arrival and cost before you select it; pickup services offer multiple locations.
  8. The final review includes payment and Complete purchase. Inspection tasks stop before submission. Preview purchases create a local simulated order; no external payment service is called.
Scope of this review

These are original modular drafts inspired by captured retail structures, not imported upstream sites. Boutique, typical and huge are authoring profiles, not a fitted distribution. The added models use a bounded projection of the pinned product-generator example; their prices and stock are local authoring offers. Each page-role selection is independent. Manual overrides are review controls; the seed button supplies reproducible illustrative choices with uniform base priors and scoped conditional permutations. Production world export, weights and SAT/weighted completion are not implemented here.
